CaptionAI — Image Captioning

ViT + GPT-2 image captioning in three styles: label, description, story

● AI & AgentsPyTorchTorchVisionTorchAudioTransformersHF Datasets
Quick Start ↓

Overview

An AI-powered web application that generates natural language captions for any image in three distinct styles: short labels, balanced descriptions, and rich story narratives. Built on Google's Vision Transformer (ViT) and OpenAI's GPT-2.
